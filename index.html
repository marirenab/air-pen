Hand gestures

🤏 Pinch thumb + index
Draw. The dot turns white while drawing.
🖐️ Open your fingers
Stop drawing and move the dot freely.
⏸️ Keep pinching and hold still
Snap a rough shape to a clean line, circle, triangle or rectangle.
☝️ Move your hand
The dot follows the point between your thumb and index finger.
